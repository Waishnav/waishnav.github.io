## Timeline

in 2023, ChatGPT launched and all of sudden we got to experience movement where machines were able to understand our language and they were able to answer our dumb questions

in 2024-2025 we experience more agentic model like sonnet 3.5 which changed how developer build software, it went from copilot suggestion to chat window inside cursor/vscode to let model do action on the behalf of it's master (human in the loop)

In early 2026, we experienced opus and gpt/codex's ability to solve and build complex software, more agentic, more intelligent tokens, yada yada, and we all concluded that programming was not supposed to be about writing it is solved now, Models are gonna write code, Humans are gonna orchestrate and manage them

## OpenClaw movement

also early 2026, we experience movement where non-technical folks came into picture, they signed up GitHub and made OpenClaw and one of the most starred repo on GitHub in just few weeks

And OpenClaw accelerated the future with it's crappy implementation of personal agent, (iron man's jarvis like assistent which will take action on the behalf of it's master)&#x20;

it showed us future where agents will be taking decision on the behalf of it's master

it showed us future where agents will be talking to another agents to get shit done

it showed the future where humans will not be needed much in knowledge/digital work

## CLI —> UI —> CLI —> UI

Back in older days when personal computer were not introduced in the market, interface for interacting with these black box machines were showing raw data through cli or some sort of logs

and these system were used by technical people only

Apple made shift in the market by introducing UI and OS and Personal computers to the market

> The computer is a bicycle for the mind
> \- Steve Jobs

Let's discuss what is UI, why it exists in the first place

Humans are visual creature, they like to see things in simplified way, human mind can process raw data and it won't make sense to em

so we built charts, dashboards, graphs to make sense of large chunks of data

and we built CRUD apps around data to mutate the data over the time of it's need (this is the era of CRUD apps)

​

So UI was needed for humans to help em summarize and make sense of data

it was invented with sole purpose of simplification&#x20;
